About us

Owner-operated, with pride in every finish

True Blue Painting is a specialist cabinet refinishing business based in North Vancouver, serving homeowners across Vancouver, Burnaby, the North Shore and the wider Lower Mainland.

With more than 20 years of hands-on experience, we've built our reputation on one simple idea: do excellent work and treat every home like our own. When you hire us, the owners do the work — the same careful hands from the first estimate to the final touch-up.

What sets us apart

Why homeowners choose True Blue

20+ years' experience

The owners do the work

No subcontractors or rotating crews. You deal with the people doing the job — consistent quality and clear communication.

Customer satisfaction first

We take genuine pride in a tidy worksite, friendly service and a finish you'll be delighted with — backed by a 3-year warranty.

Careful work, start to finish

Great results come from great preparation. We plan every project in detail, protect your home, and take the time to prep surfaces properly so the finish bonds and lasts. Nothing is rushed and nothing is left half-done — we complete the whole job.
