Accessibility Statement

Last updated:

True Blue Painting & Cabinet Refinishing is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We want all visitors to be able to find information and contact us with ease.

Our standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the internationally recognized standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and Canadian accessibility laws such as the Accessible British Columbia Act.

What we've done

  • Clear, consistent navigation and a "skip to content" link on every page.
  • Text and background colours chosen to meet AA contrast ratios.
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Keyboard-accessible menus, links, buttons and forms with visible focus.
  • Form fields with clear, associated labels and helpful instructions.
  • Responsive layouts that work on phones, tablets and desktops, and support text resizing.
  • Respect for the "reduced motion" setting in your browser or device.
  • Semantic headings and landmarks so assistive technologies can navigate the page.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment. We review our website regularly and work to improve the experience for all users as standards and technologies evolve.
