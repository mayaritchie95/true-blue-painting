Our process

How a cabinet makeover works

A clear, tidy process that's planned from the first visit to the final touch-up.

Free estimate

We visit, discuss your colour and finish, and give you a clear written quote — no obligation.

Prep & protect

We remove doors and hardware, mask surfaces, then clean and sand every surface for a lasting bond.

Prime & spray

We prime, then apply smooth sprayed coats in your chosen colour for an even, professional finish.

Reassemble & finish

We rehang doors, align everything, and complete final touch-ups so your kitchen is ready to enjoy.
