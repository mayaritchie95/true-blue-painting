Our services

Cabinet painting & refinishing

We specialise in one thing and do it exceptionally well: giving tired cabinetry a smooth, durable, brand-new finish. Every project is sprayed for a factory-quality result and available in any colour.

Kitchen cabinet painting

The heart of the home, refreshed. We transform dated or worn kitchen cabinets with a smooth sprayed finish that looks like new cabinetry — at a fraction of the cost of replacement.

Bathroom vanity painting

Update your bathroom without a full renovation. We refinish vanities and bathroom cabinetry in moisture-tough coatings and the colour of your choice.

Built-in & feature cabinetry

Media units, shelving, laundry rooms and other built-ins. We refinish feature cabinetry to match your space with the same careful prep and smooth finish.

Why refinishing

A smarter alternative to replacement

If your cabinet boxes are solid, there's usually no need to tear them out. Refinishing keeps what works and renews what you see — delivering a dramatic change with less cost, less waste and less disruption to your home.

  • Significant savings versus new cabinets
  • Days, not weeks of disruption
  • Any colour, including custom matches
  • Backed by our 3-year warranty
